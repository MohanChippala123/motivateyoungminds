Most common

You're a teen with an idea

A coach of your own, a weekly check-in, and a community of people nervously starting things alongside you. Costs nothing, ever.

  • 1-on-1 coaching matched to your interests and schedule
  • Weekly accountability that survives real life
  • Ages ~13–22 · parent consent if under 18 · always free
Join as a starter

You want to mentor

If you care more about follow-through than credentials, we want to talk. Coaches guide mindset and habits — never business plans.

  • About two hours a week, around your schedule
  • Background check and training provided (and required)
  • Age 21+ · alumni especially welcome

Donate

Your donation is somebody's first step.

We never charge young starters, so donations carry the whole program. Monthly giving is what lets us plan more than a semester ahead.

$15/mo

Keeps one teen matched with a coach for a full month, check-ins included.

$50

Trains one volunteer mentor in youth-support best practices.

$250

Brings the whole program to a classroom or after-school club.

Donation buttons are display-only right now — connect Stripe, PayPal Giving, or your provider of choice before launch.

Parents, teachers, youth leaders — this part's for you.

Know a young person with big ideas and low follow-through? Send them our way, gently. We meet them where they are: no lectures, no guilt, no “in my day.”

We also bring the program to classrooms, youth groups, and clubs — coaches come to you, and it costs the school nothing.

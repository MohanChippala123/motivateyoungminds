The idea was never the problem. Somewhere between having it and starting it, most people stall out — and almost nobody builds anything for that exact moment. So we did.

A coach in your corner, not a consultant at your door.

MotivateYoungMinds isn't an incubator, an agency, or a homework service. Nobody here asks for equity in your lemonade stand. We're coaches, mentors, and slightly older people who remember exactly how hard it is to begin.

Our whole job is the unglamorous part: the first email, the first call, the first published episode. The wins stay yours. So do the decisions.

What you actually get

Four things, no fluff

01

Motivational coaching

A real person, on a real call, who talks you down off the “what if everyone laughs” ledge. Warm, direct, allergic to inspirational posters.

02

Weekly accountability check-ins

One small goal a week, and someone who notices whether you did it. Missed it? Fine. We shrink the goal until it happens.

03

Peer community

A group chat of other people nervously starting things. Turns out “I'm the only one who hasn't begun” is a very convincing lie.

04

Habits & mindset mentorship

Procrastination, perfectionism, fear of being a beginner — mentors who've been through it, focused on follow-through rather than business plans.

So we're clear

What we do, what we won't

We do

  • Cheer, nudge, and occasionally nag — kindly
  • Break “someday” into a step you can do this week
  • Give honest advice on procrastination and fear
  • Connect you with peers who are starting too
  • Celebrate loudly when it ships

We won't

  • Build your app, brand, or business plan
  • Judge whether your idea is “good enough”
  • Call you lazy — stuck isn't a character flaw
  • Talk you out of the weird idea
  • Take credit for any of it

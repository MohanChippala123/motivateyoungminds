The idea

Direction feels different when it comes from someone close to your age.

Compass is a teen-led nonprofit for the in-between moments: the week you feel behind, the choice that feels too big, the question you don’t want to ask an adult.

Older high school students mentor younger students through three practical pillars — with training, clear boundaries, and an adult advisor in the loop.

Safety is part of the design

Peer mentors are not counselors. That boundary protects everyone.

Mentors learn how to listen, keep boundaries, and recognize when something serious needs an adult. Self-harm, abuse, or a real crisis goes straight to the adult advisor or school counselor — no exceptions.

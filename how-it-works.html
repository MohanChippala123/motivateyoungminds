How it works

Four steps. No fine print.

Free for young starters, always. Here's exactly what happens after you sign up — including the parts most programs leave out.

  1. 01
    Step 1 · two minutes

    Sign up and tell us about the idea

    Not a formal application. A couple of fields: who you are, what keeps tugging at you, when you're usually free. Half-finished thoughts welcome.

  2. 02
    Step 2 · within a week

    Meet your coach

    We match you with a trained volunteer based on interests, schedule, and general vibe. First call is low-pressure — you talk, they listen, nobody grades anything.

  3. 03
    Step 3 · first call

    Pick one small goal

    Together you break the big scary thing into one step for the week. Send one email. Sketch one page. Record one bad minute of audio. Small enough that it actually happens.

  4. 04
    Step 4 · every week after

    Check in and keep moving

    A quick weekly check-in: what happened, what's next, what's in the way. Plus the peer community for the days your motivation takes a personal day.

~30 minutes a week total · built around homework, jobs, and having a life

Behind the scenes

What a check-in actually looks like

Fifteen minutes, on your phone, zero judgment. Three questions, same order, every week:

Last week's goal — did it happen?

Did it, sort of did, or didn't touch it — all acceptable answers. A missed goal isn't a failure, it's evidence the goal was sized wrong. We adjust.

This week's goal — what's it gonna be?

One specific action that fits around your actual life. If saying it out loud makes your stomach drop, it's too big. We shrink it.

What's really in the way?

Fear, time, energy, confusion, a group project eating your soul? Your coach has heard all of it before and has practical ways through.

Fair questions
Does it cost anything?

No. Donors cover everything, so teens and young adults never pay. If you ever want to give back, donations keep it free for whoever comes next.

I don't have a fully-formed idea yet. Is that okay?

More than okay — it's the most common way people join. Coaches help you find the smallest possible version of a first step. No idea validation, no planning sessions.

Will someone build my app or write my business plan?

No, on purpose. We do motivation, mindset, and accountability only. Everything you make stays 100% yours — which is exactly why finishing feels so good.

How much time does this take?

Roughly 15–30 minutes a week: one short check-in plus whatever your weekly step takes. It's built to survive school schedules, not compete with them.

Who are the mentors? Is this safe?

All mentors are background-checked volunteers trained in youth-support guidelines. If you're under 18, a parent or guardian signs off before matching. The privacy rules are strict and boring, which is exactly how we like them.

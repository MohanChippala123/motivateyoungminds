How it works

A clear path, built around trust.

Compass pairs younger students with older teen mentors through a simple process with training, boundaries, and an adult advisor in the loop.

  1. 01
    Step 1 · two minutes

    Tell us what support you need

    Students share a little about their age, school context, and the questions they are carrying. No polished application required.

  2. 02
    Step 2 · within a week

    Meet your peer mentor

    We match a mentee with an older teen based on interests, availability, and fit. An adult advisor remains part of the support system.

  3. 03
    Step 3 · first call

    Choose a next step

    That might be a way to handle a stressful week, prepare for a conversation, or explore a future path. Small and practical is the point.

  4. 04
    Step 4 · every week after

    Check in and keep moving

    Mentors and mentees reflect, adjust, and keep building confidence. Anything serious is brought to the adult advisor or school counselor immediately.

~30 minutes a week total · built around homework, jobs, and having a life

Behind the scenes

What a check-in actually looks like

Fifteen minutes, on your phone, zero judgment. Three questions, same order, every week:

Last week's goal — did it happen?

Did it, sort of did, or didn't touch it — all acceptable answers. A missed goal isn't a failure, it's evidence the goal was sized wrong. We adjust.

This week's goal — what's it gonna be?

One specific action that fits around your actual life. If saying it out loud makes your stomach drop, it's too big. We shrink it.

What's really in the way?

Stress, uncertainty, school pressure, or a group project eating your soul? Your peer mentor can listen — and knows when to involve an adult.

Fair questions

Asked often

Does it cost anything?

No. The Compass pilot is free for students. Community supporters and school partners help cover the program so cost never decides who gets support.

I don't know what kind of support I need yet. Is that okay?

Absolutely. You can start with a question, a stressful week, or a general feeling of being stuck. Your mentor helps you name one small next step.

Can Compass help with college, careers, or trades?

Yes. Our panels and resource guides make college, careers, trades, gap years, and other routes easier to compare without pushing one “right” answer.

How much time does this take?

Roughly 15–30 minutes a week: one short check-in plus whatever your weekly step takes. It's built to survive school schedules, not compete with them.

Who are the mentors? Is this safe?

Mentors are older high school students who complete training on listening, boundaries, and escalation. An adult advisor stays in the loop, and serious concerns go straight to an adult.

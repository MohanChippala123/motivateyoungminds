Why Compass

Direction feels better when it’s shared.

Compass Teens is a teen-led nonprofit connecting younger students with older peers for resilience, well-being, and practical direction.

The problem we see

Students are asked to make big choices before they have a place to ask small questions.

High school is full of decisions that look simple from the outside: making friends, handling stress, choosing classes, and imagining life after graduation.

Those decisions feel more manageable when advice comes from someone who remembers them. Compass creates that connection without pretending a peer mentor is a counselor or an adult professional.

Our lane

Peer support with real guardrails.

We do

  • Match younger students with older teen mentors
  • Run resilience and well-being workshops
  • Make college, career, trades, and other paths visible
  • Train mentors in listening, boundaries, and escalation
  • Keep an adult advisor in the loop

We do not

  • Provide therapy, counseling, or crisis response
  • Allow private or unobservable mentor communication
  • Leave a mentor to handle a serious disclosure alone
  • Promise one “right” path after high school
  • Ask students to solve everything by themselves

The founding model

Teen-led, adult-supported.

STUDENT LEADERSHIP

Real ownership

Teen founders run the program, recruit the team, shape the pillars, and learn by doing.

ADULT ADVISOR

Responsible support

A parent, teacher, counselor, or youth leader handles legal, safeguarding, and school-level responsibilities.

PILOT FIRST

Start small

Compass can begin as a school club or informal group, then build a track record before considering larger nonprofit structures.

A clear safety rule

Peer mentors are not counselors.

Anything involving self-harm, abuse, or a real crisis goes straight to the adult advisor or school counselor. No exceptions. That protects mentees and mentors alike.

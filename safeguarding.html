Safeguarding

Safety isn't a feature. It's the frame.

Coaching minors is a responsibility we take more seriously than anything else on this site. Here's exactly how it works. Last updated August 2026.

Every mentor is vetted

All volunteer mentors pass a criminal background check, complete youth-protection training, and provide references before their first match. Re-screening happens annually.

Communication is observable

Check-ins happen on monitored channels — video calls or program group chats. Mentors are prohibited from moving conversations into private DMs or personal social accounts. Parents of under-18s can view any check-in record on request.

Consent before matching

No one under 18 is matched with a mentor until a parent or guardian has reviewed the program rules and given written consent. Anyone under 13 isn't eligible for 1-on-1 mentoring and is directed to group resources only.

Reporting is easy and taken seriously

Anything uncomfortable — a message, a comment, a pattern — goes to hello@motivateyoungminds.org with the subject "safety." A designated safeguarding lead reviews every report within 24 hours. We follow mandatory reporting obligations and cooperate with authorities where legally required.

Consequences are immediate

A mentor flagged for a safeguarding concern is suspended from all contact pending review. We would rather lose a volunteer than say "we'll look into it."

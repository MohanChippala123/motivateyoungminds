Privacy

Your data, in plain English.

We work with minors, so this matters more here than most places. No legal fog machine — just the facts of what we collect and what happens to it. Last updated August 2026.

What we collect

When you message us or sign up: your name (first name is fine), an email address, your age range, and whatever you tell us about the support you need. Mentors and advisors only keep the brief notes needed to coordinate a safe check-in.

Why we need it

Only to run the program: matching you with a mentor, scheduling check-ins, and sending the weekly nudge you asked for. Nothing else. We do not profile you, and we do not advertise to you.

What we never do

Sell or share your data with advertisers. Post your personal story publicly without written permission. Contact you outside program channels. Keep your records after you leave and ask us to delete them.

Minors

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. For anyone under 18, a parent or guardian consents before a mentor is matched, and can view or delete their teen's records at any time.

Cookies & tracking

This site sets no tracking cookies, runs no analytics, and embeds no ads. The site works fine with every blocker awake. Email open-tracking is also off by default.

Deletion & questions

Email mohan0512vittal@gmail.com with the subject "delete my data" and it's gone within 7 days — account, notes, and check-in history. Questions get a human answer, not a policy link.
